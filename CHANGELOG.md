Changelog

All notable changes to SECAV-O are documented in this file.

Earlier versions were internal and were not published. Version 0.3.0 is the first public release.

[0.3.0] - 2026-10-28 — First public release
Ontology and validation
Core SECAV-O ontology for engineering activities, artifacts, competencies, evidence, AI assistance and human validation, with a stable SECAV-O namespace and explicit owl:imports declarations.
Coaching-governance ontology extension covering project-planning participation, quality advocacy, dissent and escalation, annual and sprint planning, risk management, metrics, ethics and alignment.
Ontology terms for learning exercises, review roles, participant-role traceability in review sessions, and pessimistic defect-prevention techniques.
SHACL validation shapes for the core ontology and the coaching-governance extension.
Coaching governance and guidance
Governance documents for the coach role profile, team lifecycle, coaching cycle and institutional alignment.
Guidance for annual planning, sprint planning, retrospectives, risk governance, ethics, metrics and competency growth.
Guidance for robust process criteria, internal audits, CMMI mapping, coach ethics, observation checklists and review-quality practices.
Clear distinctions between coaching-program risks and project risks, and between evidence and measurement and coaching-intervention concepts.
Templates, examples and exercises
Templates and worked examples for design reviews, coaching sprints, risk analysis, onboarding, sprint planning, retrospectives and competency assessment.
Machine-readable example records for planning and risk governance.
The first learning exercise package, exercises/java-standard-deviation/, with its ontology instance in examples/java-standard-deviation-exercise.ttl.
A pilot protocol (pilot/pilot-protocol.md, draft).
Repository and documentation
README, glossary and conceptual architecture documentation reflecting the current structure of the framework.
CITATION.cff and release metadata matching the public repository URL and identity.
Consistency fixes across ontology references, SHACL declarations, examples, glossary and validation metadata.
Status
This is an experimental prototype. It is not represented as complete, standardized, independently validated, or adopted by third parties.
